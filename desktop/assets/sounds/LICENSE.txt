Clipfarm Studio interface sounds: intro.ogg, check.ogg, ready.ogg, start.ogg

Original work, generated from scratch by desktop/scripts/make-sounds.py
(sine oscillators, filtered noise and a synthetic reverb; no samples, no
third-party recordings). Created for Clipfarm Studio in November 2026.

Owned by the Clipfarm Studio project and dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so they can be
used without attribution and regenerated at any time with the script.
